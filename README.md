# Carvix Raw — one-knob MIDI sample mangler

![CarvixRaw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/CarvixRaw/main/carvixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**Drop a sample, play a key, twist one knob — instant creative resampling.**

CarvixRaw is a MIDI-driven instrument, not an audio insert. Place it on a MIDI/instrument track and play it from your keyboard or a MIDI clip. It sounds the moment it loads thanks to a built-in one-shot, or drop your own sample onto it.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

## 🎛️ What It Does
Carvix Raw is a 4-voice sampler you play with MIDI. Insert it on a MIDI/instrument track or as a Software Instrument, then trigger it from your keyboard or a MIDI clip. It ships with a built-in one-shot so it makes sound the instant you press a key — or **drag and drop your own audio file** onto the plug-in to play that instead. Notes pitch the sample across the keyboard (root C4).

One big **MANGLE** knob progressively wrecks the playback as you turn it up: pitch-down → reverse → stutter → bitcrush. The brand **Fire Head** graphic is a live preview of the mangle — it warps, flips, splits, and crushes exactly as you hear it, so you can dial in the sound by eye as well as ear.

That's the whole instrument: play it with MIDI, pick a sample, and ride the one knob.
